TEA Kids supports everyday autonomy, predictability and emotional regulation for autistic children and teenagers, with simple language and emojis.

Features:
• Visual routines with one step at a time, a gentle timer, pause and extra time.
• Emotion check-ins and a diary of recent entries.
• Help cards with messages to show someone and optional phone/SMS actions.
• Calming activity cards, favorites and guided breathing.
• Light, dark and system themes.
• An Android home-screen widget that opens the help card.

User entries and preferences are stored locally. Core activities work offline; calling or sending SMS uses the phone's services. The interface is currently in Brazilian Portuguese.
